Corporate values

What governs XARU — in every mandate

Stewardship

Every asset, project and family is treated as patrimony under care — never as inventory.

One responsibility

One structure answers for the whole route — no hand-offs, no diluted accountability.

Confidentiality

Discretion is a working method: teasers without sensitive data, NDA before disclosure, private channels.

Rigour & compliance

Verification before publication; KYC / AML through regulated channels where applicable; nothing affirmed that has not been verified.

Continuity

XARU remains after the transaction — operation, administration and follow-through, year after year.

A distinction we keep deliberately: health and wellness, ancestral culture, eco-friendly practice, innovation and sustainable development are the five narrative pillars of the ASHIMA project — the language of one signature development. The values above are the corporate values of XARU HOME itself.

Entities & scope

Under which structure XARU operates

XARU HOME operates under NEXARU GLOBAL, licensed in the United Arab Emirates. Regulated activities are provided through the corresponding entities or authorised partners in each jurisdiction.

Every proposal states, in writing, which entity or partner acts, in which jurisdiction and in which capacity. What cannot be stated in writing is not offered.

Governance & standards

Power is demonstrated, not declared

Every XARU mandate — residential, territorial, capital or corporate — runs under the same standards:

  • Confidentiality as method — teasers without sensitive data; disclosure only under NDA
  • Due diligence and verified mandates before anything is published
  • KYC / AML through regulated channels where the activity requires it
  • Information protection — owners, coordinates, financials and licences are never exposed
  • Legal and tax coordination with specialised counsel in each jurisdiction
  • A defined regulatory scope — regulated services through authorised entities and partners, stated in writing
  • Documented sequence in every process — intake, verification, NDA, review, execution, follow-through

Depending on the project, jurisdiction, feasibility and mandate, XARU may act as adviser, structurer, integrator, sponsor, manager or participant. XARU designs, integrates and coordinates financial and technological infrastructure through authorised entities and partners where the activity requires it.
