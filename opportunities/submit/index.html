XARU works as a principal on both sides of the table. Two routes, one standard of verification — tell us which side you are on.

Route A — Submit a project

I have an asset or a project and I need capital, a developer, an operator, a buyer, a partner, distribution or restructuring.

  1. 01Describe the asset or project privately
  2. 02Preliminary review by the relevant division
  3. 03NDA where the information requires it
  4. 04Verification of title and counterparty
  5. 05A structured process begins — never a listing

Route B — Deploy capital

I have capital or capability and I am seeking projects, land, operating assets, distressed opportunities, joint ventures, hotel developments or private mandates.

  1. 01State your criteria and indicative ticket
  2. 02Preliminary review of fit against live mandates
  3. 03NDA and KYC / AML where applicable
  4. 04Verification of capital or capability
  5. 05Curated opportunities through the Private Deal Room
