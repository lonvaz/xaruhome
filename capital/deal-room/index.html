Private Deal Room

  1. Home
  2. Capital & Transactions
  3. Private Deal Room

Some opportunities are too large, too sensitive or too strategic for a public listing. The Private Deal Room lets them exist — visible enough to be real, protected enough to be safe.

Every confidential opportunity travels the same nine-step route. No step is skipped; no information is released ahead of its step.

The private route

Nine steps, in order

  1. 01

    Public teaser

    The opportunity is announced without owners, coordinates, financials or price.

  2. 02

    Access request

    The interested party requests private access through the form below.

  3. 03

    Identity and profile verification

    Who is asking, in what capacity, and with what capability.

  4. 04

    NDA

    A non-disclosure agreement is executed before any sensitive detail moves.

  5. 05

    KYC / AML where applicable

    Completed through regulated channels when the transaction requires it.

  6. 06

    Internal approval

    XARU reviews the request against the mandate before opening anything.

  7. 07

    Data room

    Documents, financials and licences are released inside a controlled environment.

  8. 08

    Adviser assignment

    A dedicated adviser carries the party through the transaction.

  9. 09

    Transaction process

    Negotiation, structure and closing, managed under one responsibility.

What is protected

What never appears in public

  • Owners' identities
  • Coordinates and exact locations
  • Financial statements
  • Licences and permits
  • Legal documents
  • Sensitive price
  • Strategic information
